# Vocal Compass maintenance contract

Keep the product simple and the evidence stronger than the marketing.

- Preserve four primary destinations: Today, Practice, Progress and Guide. Everyday actions should not require reading the research library.
- Any research claim must link a primary source in `src/core/science/evidence.ts`, with population/task, findings, limits, access depth and review date. Update the rendered Guide and relevant docs together. Do not invent sample sizes, effect sizes, guaranteed outcomes or universal absence-of-evidence claims.
- Keep observation, user report, inference and proposed training strategy distinct. Pitch is not intention, vocal register, strain, artistry or a diagnosis.
- Do not convert relative pitch-error reductions or standardized effect sizes into percentage-point note-accuracy promises. Do not reinstate a three-semitone minimum detectable change or a VFE-equivalence claim without direct validation of this instrument/protocol.
- Only provenance-complete matched check-ins can enter the longitudinal comparison. Keep every captured first attempt, no result-based retries, no live target/pitch feedback, no unlogged replays. Deleting a member invalidates the complete block. Never silently classify legacy records as controlled tests.
- Show denominators, unscored coverage and missing values. No-data is not zero error. Intervals are descriptive and not proof of learning, causality or calibrated device noise.
- Keep microphone/cue lifecycle cancellable. Playback must not overlap scored voice capture without a validated rejection/alignment pipeline. A failed save must not duplicate an attempt or discard its review state.
- Voice readiness gates precede singing; stop must stop capture/playback. Do not claim the software can hear strain or certify safety. Range exploration is optional and not a higher-belt treatment.
- Synthetic fixtures, design-preview mocks and sample progress must never enter the production entrypoint or masquerade as personal progress. Never embed a user's private goal or profile in this public repository.
- Run `npm test` and `npm run build`, inspect mobile/desktop rendering, and record exact scope. Changes to metric definitions require tests and versioning. Preserve old data rather than rewriting its meaning.
- Read `docs/release-validation.md` before release. Automated and isolated-renderer tests do not complete the real-voice, real-device, account-sync or deployed-browser gates. Do not mark them passed without evidence.
